About

I am a senior executive focused on helping organizations navigate complex transformation with clarity, structure, and practical judgment. My work has spanned enterprise systems, operating model change, data governance, reporting, and large-scale business technology initiatives in complex environments.

Over time, my interests have expanded beyond transformation delivery toward a broader question: how do powerful technologies, especially AI, change work, institutions, incentives, and public life? I explore that question two ways — by writing about it publicly, and by building AI applications myself so my views stay grounded in what the technology can actually do.
